# Training / Timeskip Arc Template

> Model beats: DBZ's **Hyperbolic Time Chamber**, Piccolo/Gohan wilderness training, Yu Yu
> Hakusho's Genkai apprenticeship, Naruto's 2.5‑year timeskip. A spin‑out of the
> [Toonami/Shōnen template](./toonami-shonen-powerup-arc.md).

## The promise
Buy massive character growth without spending equivalent story time — and set up a technique
that pays off later, withheld from the reader until the moment it matters most.

## The beats
1. **Trigger** — a defeat, or a countdown ("the enemy arrives in X days/years").
2. **Isolation + compressed time** — a device that buys growth cheaply. The Hyperbolic Time
   Chamber's gimmick (1 year inside = 1 day outside, plus multiplied gravity) is the
   archetype; a series timeskip does the same at a larger scale.
3. **Mentor imparts a discipline, not just a move** — control, patience, a philosophy, not
   only a technique.
4. **The new technique is set up but withheld** — trained here, unveiled at the arc's climax
   so the payoff lands mid‑fight, not mid‑training.
5. **Visible transformation of the hero** — new outfit, scars, calmer demeanor: a design
   change that signals "leveled up" at a glance.
6. **Cost/lesson** — the training reframes the hero's *values*, not just their stats.

## Craft notes
- **Withholding is the whole trick.** If you show the new technique being learned in full
  during training, you spend its surprise. Show the *cost* of training it, cut away before
  mastery, then reveal mastery in the rematch.
- **Training montages compress time visually** (screen) or via **section breaks + summary
  narration** (prose) — don't narrate every training day; pick 2–3 vivid beats.
- **The mentor's method should teach the story's theme**, not just a fighting style — e.g.
  "patience," "let go of anger," "trust your team" — so the technique and the character arc
  land in the same beat.

## Variations & subversions
- **Training fails / backfires** — the new technique has a flaw the hero hasn't found yet,
  discovered painfully in the rematch (raises stakes further).
- **No mentor available** — solo, environmental training (survival itself as teacher).
- **Training happens off‑page entirely** — a hard timeskip with zero training beats shown,
  relying purely on the "visible transformation" reveal for impact (higher risk, can feel
  unearned if the audience wasn't warned a skip was coming).

## Blank template
```text
TRIGGER (defeat / countdown):
ISOLATION DEVICE (how time/growth is compressed):
MENTOR:                     DISCIPLINE TAUGHT (not just a move):
NEW TECHNIQUE (withheld until):
VISIBLE TRANSFORMATION (design change):
THEME the training teaches:
COST — what does mastering it cost?
REVEAL SCENE — where/how it's unveiled:
```
