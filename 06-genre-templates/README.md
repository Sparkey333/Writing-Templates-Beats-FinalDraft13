# 06 — Genre Templates

Where [`02-story-structure/`](../02-story-structure/) gives universal spines, these are
**genre‑specific obligatory beats and conventions** — the promises a genre makes to its
audience, and the shape that keeps that promise.

## The flagship (your request)
⭐ **[toonami-shonen-powerup-arc.md](./toonami-shonen-powerup-arc.md)** — the '90s/2000s
Toonami/Adult Swim shōnen action‑serial template (DBZ, Yu Yu Hakusho, and kin): power
escalation, the arc cadence, and the transformation beat.
