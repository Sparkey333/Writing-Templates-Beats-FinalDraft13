# Space Opera Template

> Mythic stakes on a galactic canvas. Genre‑pattern analysis (the *Star Wars*/*Dune*/*Foundation*
> tradition), not text from any specific work.

## The promise
The scale of epic fantasy transposed to the stars — factions, empires, and destinies — with
the added texture of technology, exploration, and (often) a found‑family crew at human scale
inside the vast plot.

## Obligatory beats/conventions
1. **A galaxy‑scale stakes frame** — empire vs. rebellion, ancient order vs. insurgency,
   a civilization‑ending threat — established early, even if the plot starts small.
2. **A human‑scale entry point** — space opera almost always grounds the vast stakes through
   a small crew/family/ship the audience can track intimately (contrast:
   [epic‑fantasy multi‑POV](./epic-fantasy-braided-multipov.md), which spreads across many
   POVs instead of centering one crew).
3. **Politics with texture** — factions have real, competing goods (not just good‑vs‑evil);
   see [worldbuilding § Politics & Power](../04-worldbuilding/worldbuilding-master-questionnaire.md).
4. **A technology/magic system with rules** — FTL travel, psychic powers, ancient artifacts —
   apply [Sanderson's Laws](../04-worldbuilding/sanderson-laws-of-magic.md) directly; space
   opera tech is functionally a magic system and benefits from the same rigor.
5. **A mythic throughline** — prophecy, destiny, or a legendary order (Jedi‑style) that gives
   personal stakes a cosmic register.
6. **Escalating scope** — local conflict → planetary → systemic → galactic, mirroring the
   [Toonami power‑scaling](./toonami-shonen-powerup-arc.md) principle of raising stakes'
   *scope*, not just difficulty.
7. **A found‑family crew** — the emotional core; often more important to reader attachment
   than the galactic plot itself.

## Craft notes
- **Ground the vastness in objects and rituals** — a specific ship, a specific blade, a
  specific song — the [Tolkien iceberg](../04-worldbuilding/tolkien-method.md) technique
  applied to hardware and culture instead of just history.
- **Faction motivations need to survive scrutiny** — "empire is evil because evil" reads
  thin; give every major faction a real want that makes sense from the inside.
- **Don't let scale replace stakes** — a bigger explosion isn't automatically higher stakes;
  keep the human‑scale crew's personal cost visible even during galaxy‑level climaxes.

## Variations & subversions
- **Anti‑war space opera** — subvert the mythic‑destiny framing; show the cost/horror
  underneath the "chosen one saves the galaxy" convention.
- **Political space opera with no single hero** — closer to
  [braided multi‑POV](./epic-fantasy-braided-multipov.md); factions themselves are the
  "characters."
- **Small‑scale space opera** — one ship, one crew, galaxy‑stakes only in the background
  (bottle‑episode structure).

```text
GALAXY-SCALE STAKES:
HUMAN-SCALE ENTRY POINT (the crew/ship/family):
FACTIONS (min. 3, each with a real internal logic):
  Faction | Goal | Why it makes sense from the inside
  ___     | ___  | ___
  ___     | ___  | ___
TECH/MAGIC SYSTEM — apply Sanderson's Laws (see magic-system-design-template.md):
MYTHIC THROUGHLINE (prophecy/order/destiny):
SCOPE ESCALATION: local → ___ → ___ → galactic
FOUND-FAMILY CREW:
```
