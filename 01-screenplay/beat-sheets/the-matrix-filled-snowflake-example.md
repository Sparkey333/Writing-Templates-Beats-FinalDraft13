# Worked Example: The FD13 × Snowflake Master Template, filled out on *The Matrix*

This is a **fully filled‑out example** of the
[FD13 × Snowflake Master Template](../templates/final-draft-snowflake-master-template.md),
using *The Matrix* (1999) as the practice subject — so you can see every step of the
template actually used, start to finish.

> **Important:** everything below is **original structural analysis**, written from memory
> of the film's plot in our own words — not the screenplay. It's built to *teach the
> template*, not to stand in for the real script. Read it side‑by‑side with the real script
> ([IMSDb](https://imsdb.com/scripts/Matrix,-The.html) ·
> [Script Slug](https://www.scriptslug.com/script/the-matrix-1999)) and the
> [Matrix beat sheet](./the-matrix-beat-sheet.md) for the Save‑the‑Cat‑mapped version of the

## Snowflake Step 1 — One‑Sentence Summary
```text
LOGLINE: A hacker discovers his world is a simulation and must choose to fight
the machines that built it.
```

## Snowflake Step 2 — One‑Paragraph Summary
```text
SETUP: A hacker known online as "Neo" senses something is deeply wrong with reality
and searches for a mysterious figure who might explain why.

DISASTER 1 (~25%): Neo is captured and nearly implanted with a tracking device by
agents of the system, then rescued and told the truth: his entire life has been a
simulation, and the real world is a ruined future where machines farm humans for
energy. He can never return to his old life.

DISASTER 2 (~50%): Neo learns from a prophetic figure (the Oracle) that he is
probably NOT "the One" the resistance has been waiting for — undercutting his
growing confidence right as the mission's danger peaks.

DISASTER 3 (~75%): A trusted member of the crew betrays the team to the machines;
several crew members are killed or captured, including the mentor figure,
Morpheus, who is taken prisoner by the system's agents.

ENDING: Neo chooses to go back into the simulation to save Morpheus rather than
run, dies and is revived by an act of love and belief, and emerges with full
mastery over the simulation's rules — ending the film as a direct threat to the
system that built the world he grew up in.
```

## Snowflake Step 3 — Character Summary Sheets
```text
── NEO (protagonist) ──
ONE-LINE ARC: A disaffected hacker becomes someone who believes enough in a
  cause — and in himself — to bend the rules of reality.
MOTIVATION (abstract want): To understand why his life feels wrong.
GOAL (concrete want): To find "Morpheus" and get an answer to "what is the Matrix?"
CONFLICT: He doesn't believe he's special; the people around him insist he is.
EPIPHANY: Belief is a choice, and choosing it changes what's possible.
STORYLINE (his paragraph): Neo starts as a passive searcher, is forced into an
  active role by the truth he's shown, doubts himself hardest right when he's
  told he might not be "the One," and only becomes powerful once he stops
  waiting to be proven special and instead simply chooses to act.

── MORPHEUS (mentor) ──
ONE-LINE ARC: A true believer's faith is tested and ultimately validated.
MOTIVATION: To free humanity from the simulation.
GOAL: To find and confirm "the One" who can end the war.
CONFLICT: His absolute certainty makes him a target, and nearly gets him killed.
EPIPHANY: ⚑ (does Morpheus change, or is he the story's flat/steadfast anchor?
  Worth deciding deliberately — see character-arc-template.md on flat arcs.)

── TRINITY (B-story / love interest) ──
ONE-LINE ARC: A hardened operative allows herself to hope again.
MOTIVATION: Loyalty to the cause and to Morpheus.
GOAL: Protect Neo, verify he's really "the One."
CONFLICT: A prophecy told her she'd fall for "the One" — she resists what that
  means until it matters most.
EPIPHANY: Her belief (spoken aloud) is the literal mechanism of Neo's climax.

── CYPHER (fake-ally opponent) ──
ONE-LINE ARC: A man who has seen too much truth chooses comfortable lies instead.
MOTIVATION: Exhaustion; he wants his simulated old life back, ignorance included.
GOAL: Cut a deal with the system to be reinserted, in exchange for betraying the
  crew.
CONFLICT: His cynicism vs. Neo's growing faith — the story's thematic foil.
EPIPHANY: none — his refusal to change is the point (see redemption-arc.md for
  the shape he explicitly does NOT follow).

⚑ Repeat this block for Agent Smith and the Oracle before drafting — flagged in the
Focus Tab below.
```

## Snowflake Step 4 — One‑Page Synopsis (excerpt — expand each Step‑2 sentence)
```text
[SETUP, expanded] Neo lives a double life: mild-mannered software employee by day,
restless hacker by night, chasing rumors of a man named Morpheus who might have
the answer to a question that's haunted him for years — "what is the Matrix?"
Strange, impossible things keep happening around him that the world insists he
ignore.

[DISASTER 1, expanded] Contacted at last by Trinity and Morpheus's crew, Neo is
warned that agents are closing in — and they are, nearly capturing and implanting
him with a tracking device in a scene that plays like a nightmare made real. Freed
and extracted, he's offered a choice — a pill that shows him a comforting lie, or
one that shows him the truth. He chooses truth, and wakes into a ruined real world
he never knew existed: humanity enslaved, farmed for energy by machines, while
their minds are kept docile inside the simulated "Matrix." There is no going back
to the world he grew up in — it was never real.

... ⚑ (continue expanding Disaster 2, Disaster 3, and the Ending to full
paragraphs before drafting — left as an exercise / flagged in Focus Tab.)
```

## Snowflake Step 7 — Character Bibles
Full dossiers → use [character-dossier-template.md](../../05-character/character-dossier-template.md)
per character. Fast‑version excerpt for the two leads:
```text
NEO — late 20s, unremarkable by design (the point is that anyone could be him) —
  Want: answers. Need: belief in himself. Flaw: passivity/self-doubt. Ghost: a
  lifetime of sensing something's wrong and being told he's crazy for it.

MORPHEUS — older, commanding, calm — Want: to find the One. Need: ⚑ (arc
  undecided — see Step 3 note above). Flaw: absolute certainty can blind him to
  risk (nearly walks into Cypher's betrayal unaware). Ghost: has been searching,
  and been wrong before, many times.
```

## Snowflake Step 9 — Scene Narrative Briefs (one worked example)
```text
SCENE #: 5 (Oracle's apartment)
CONFLICT: Neo wants confirmation he's "the One"; the Oracle withholds it.
SETBACK/TURN: She tells him he has the gift, but isn't the One — then, almost as
  an aside, warns him about a choice regarding Morpheus's life. Both statements
  will be recontextualized by the ending.
POV GOAL ENTERING/EXITING: Enters seeking certainty; exits with more doubt AND a
  hidden piece of foreshadowing he doesn't understand yet.
```
