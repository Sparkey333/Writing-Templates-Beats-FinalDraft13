# World‑Building Master Questionnaire

A deep, copy‑paste template for building a secondary world. **Don't answer all of it** — use
[Sanderson's Third Law](./sanderson-laws-of-magic.md) (go deep where the story lives) and the
[Tolkien iceberg](./tolkien-method.md) (know more than you show). Star ★ the ~20 answers that
actually touch your plot; the rest is submerged depth.

> Flag any `⚑` you're unsure about — those feed the [app's To‑Do / focus tab](../app/README.md).

```text
WORLD NAME:                         GENRE / TONE:
STORY THIS WORLD SERVES (logline):
CORE "WHAT IF" (the one speculative premise):

── 1. PHYSICS & COSMOLOGY ─────────────────────────────
[ ] Is this Earth, alt-Earth, or secondary world?
[ ] Creation myth (what the cultures BELIEVE happened):
[ ] Cosmology: gods? afterlife? are they real in-world?  ⚑
[ ] Natural laws that differ from ours:

── 2. MAGIC / TECH SYSTEM (see magic-system-design-template.md) ──
[ ] Source / who can use it:
[ ] Rules (powers):
[ ] Limitations & cost (2nd Law — spend time here):
[ ] Hard or soft (1st Law)?
[ ] 3rd-Law ripples → economy / war / religion / politics / class:

── 3. GEOGRAPHY ───────────────────────────────────────
[ ] Map drawn? (draw it first — let it constrain travel/plot)
[ ] Major regions & what defines each:
[ ] Climate/biomes; how geography shaped cultures:
[ ] Resources people fight over:

── 4. HISTORY (the iceberg) ───────────────────────────
[ ] Timeline of "ages"/eras, 3–5 events each (most never shown):
[ ] The last great war/cataclysm & its living scars:
[ ] Myths vs. what REALLY happened (dramatic irony fuel):  ⚑

── 5. PEOPLES & CULTURES (repeat per culture) ─────────
[ ] Name / values / taboos:
[ ] Social structure & class mobility:
[ ] Rites of passage, marriage, death customs:
[ ] Language & naming rules (Tolkien method):
[ ] How they see outsiders / the other cultures:

── 6. POLITICS & POWER ────────────────────────────────
[ ] Who rules & by what claim (blood/force/vote/faith/wealth)?
[ ] Factions & their goals (your antagonists live here):  ⚑
[ ] Laws, crime, punishment:
[ ] Current tensions the story will detonate:

── 7. ECONOMY & DAILY LIFE ────────────────────────────
[ ] Currency, trade, who's rich/poor and why:
[ ] What an ordinary person eats/wears/does in a day:
[ ] Technology level (and why it stalled/advanced there):

── 8. RELIGION & BELIEF ───────────────────────────────
[ ] Dominant faith(s), practices, clergy, heresies:
[ ] How belief shapes law, war, daily choices:

── 9. THE SENSES (make it real) ───────────────────────
[ ] What does the capital SMELL like? SOUND like?
[ ] Signature food, drink, music, architecture, fashion:

── 10. STORY HOOKS (why any of this matters) ──────────
★ Which 3 world facts create the central conflict?
★ Which world detail will pay off at the climax?
★ What does the world PROMISE the reader (tone/wonder)?

⚑ OPEN WORLD DECISIONS (→ focus tab):
  [ ]
  [ ]
```
