**The two masters, in one line each:**
- **Tolkien** — go for *depth* and history; know far more than you show (the iceberg).
- **Sanderson** — go for *rigor* and consequence; limitations beat powers, and expand what
  you have before adding more.

**Don't over‑build.** Worlds serve stories. Answer the ~20 questions that touch your plot
(star them in the questionnaire), imply the rest, and go write.
