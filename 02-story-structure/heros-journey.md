# The Hero's Journey (The Monomyth)

> A hero ventures forth from the world of common day into a region of supernatural wonder…
> — Joseph Campbell, *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* (1949)

The deep pattern under most myth, fantasy, and adventure. Two versions matter: Campbell's
**17 stages** (anthropological) and Christopher Vogler's **12 stages** (the screenwriter's
condensation, from *The Writer's Journey*, 1992).

## When to use it
- Adventure, fantasy, coming‑of‑age, "chosen one" and quest stories.
- When the plot *is* the character's transformation from ordinary to extraordinary.
- Watch for cliché: the pattern is so familiar that hitting every beat literally can feel
  rote. Use it as a diagnostic, then subvert.

## Campbell's 17 stages
**Departure:** 1) Call to Adventure · 2) Refusal of the Call · 3) Supernatural Aid ·
4) Crossing the First Threshold · 5) Belly of the Whale.
**Initiation:** 6) Road of Trials · 7) Meeting with the Goddess · 8) Woman as Temptress ·
9) Atonement with the Father · 10) Apotheosis · 11) The Ultimate Boon.
**Return:** 12) Refusal of the Return · 13) The Magic Flight · 14) Rescue from Without ·
15) Crossing the Return Threshold · 16) Master of Two Worlds · 17) Freedom to Live.

## Vogler's 12 stages (the practical version)
| # | Stage | Act | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ordinary World** | 1 | Normal life; establish stakes & flaws. |
| 2 | **Call to Adventure** | 1 | A problem disrupts the ordinary world. |
| 3 | **Refusal of the Call** | 1 | The hero balks out of fear. |
| 4 | **Meeting the Mentor** | 1 | A guide gives advice, training, or gifts. |
| 5 | **Crossing the First Threshold** | 1→2 | Hero commits; enters the Special World. |
| 6 | **Tests, Allies, Enemies** | 2 | Learns the rules; makes friends and foes. |
| 7 | **Approach to the Inmost Cave** | 2 | Prep for the central challenge. |
| 8 | **The Ordeal** | 2 (mid) | Life‑or‑death crisis; greatest fear faced. |
| 9 | **Reward (Seizing the Sword)** | 2 | Survives; takes the prize/knowledge. |
| 10 | **The Road Back** | 3 | Recommits; heads home; pressure resumes. |
| 11 | **Resurrection** | 3 | Climactic final test; rebirth proves change. |
| 12 | **Return with the Elixir** | 3 | Returns transformed, bringing something back. |

## Worked example — *Star Wars: A New Hope*
Ordinary World (moisture farm) → Call (Leia's message) → Refusal ("I can't get involved") →
Mentor (Obi‑Wan) → Threshold (Mos Eisley/leaving Tatooine) → Tests/Allies/Enemies (cantina,
Han, Chewie) → Inmost Cave (the Death Star) → Ordeal (trash compactor / Obi‑Wan's death) →
Reward (rescue Leia, the plans) → Road Back (escape, TIE chase) → Resurrection (trench run,
"use the Force") → Return with the Elixir (medal ceremony, the hero reborn).
