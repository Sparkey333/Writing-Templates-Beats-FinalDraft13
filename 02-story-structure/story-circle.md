# Dan Harmon's Story Circle — 8 Steps

> A character is in a zone of comfort, but they want something…
> — Dan Harmon (creator of *Community*, *Rick and Morty*)

Harmon's radical simplification of the Hero's Journey into **8 steps arranged on a circle**.
The top half is order/comfort/the known; the bottom half is chaos/the unknown. Steps 1–4
descend into the unknown; steps 5–8 climb back — changed.

## When to use it
- Character‑driven stories and **episodic** TV (it's built to repeat every episode).
- Fast outlining — you can circle‑map an idea in five minutes.
- Great for making sure a plot actually *changes the protagonist*.

## The 8 steps
1. **You** — a character in a zone of comfort (establish protagonist + status quo).
2. **Need** — but they want something (a desire/lack pulls them out).
3. **Go** — they enter an unfamiliar situation (cross the threshold).
4. **Search** — they adapt to it (trials, learning, the road of trials).
5. **Find** — they get what they wanted (the midpoint "find").
6. **Take** — they pay a heavy price for it (cost/sacrifice; consequences).
7. **Return** — they come back to their familiar situation.
8. **Change** — having been transformed (the circle closes, but higher/lower than it began).

```
            1 YOU
     8 CHANGE   2 NEED
  7 RETURN         3 GO
     6 TAKE     4 SEARCH
            5 FIND
   (order above the line ── chaos below)
```

## The two axes (why it works)
- **Vertical:** conscious (top) ↔ unconscious (bottom). The hero descends into the unknown
  and drags something back up.
- **1↔5 and 3↔7 rhyme:** the *Need* (2) is answered by the *Find* (5); the *Go* (3) is
  mirrored by the *Return* (7). Use those rhymes to make the ending pay off the setup.

## Worked example — *Rick and Morty*, "Pilot" (compressed)
You (Morty, failing school) → Need (to be taken seriously / adventure) → Go (interdimensional
trip with Rick) → Search (mega‑seeds, alien customs house) → Find (gets the seeds / survives)
→ Take (has to smuggle them the worst way; trauma) → Return (back home/school) → Change
(Morty is subtly hardened, complicit in Rick's world).

## Blank template
```text
TITLE:
1. YOU (comfort zone) —
2. NEED (the want/lack) —
3. GO (into the unknown) —
4. SEARCH (adapt / trials) —
5. FIND (get what they wanted) —
6. TAKE (the price paid) —
7. RETURN (back to the familiar) —
8. CHANGE (how they're different) —

CHECK: Does #5 answer #2? Does #7 mirror #3? Is #8 a real change from #1?
```
