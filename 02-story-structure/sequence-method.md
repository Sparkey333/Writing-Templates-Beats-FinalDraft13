# The Sequence Method — 8 Sequences

> Don't write a 2‑hour movie. Write eight 15‑minute movies that connect.
> — after **Frank Daniel** (USC); codified by his student **Paul Joseph Gulino**,
> *Screenwriting: The Sequence Approach* (2004).

A film is built from ~8 sequences of **~10–15 pages** each — a legacy of the silent era,
when movies were assembled from ~8 physical reels. Each sequence is a **mini‑movie** with its
own goal, its own beginning/middle/end, and its own turning point ("curtain").

## When to use it
- Fixing a saggy Act 2 — it breaks the daunting middle 50% into four manageable chunks.
- Feature screenplays and long‑form TV.

## The 8 sequences (roughly 2 + 4 + 2 across the acts)
| Seq | Act | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1 | Status quo + inciting incident; pose the central tension. |
| **B** | 1 | Predicament & lock‑in; hero commits to the goal (end of Act 1). |
| **C** | 2a | First attempt via the obvious approach — which creates new problems. |
| **D** | 2a | Raise the stakes; escalate toward the midpoint. |
| **E** | 2b | Midpoint reversal; the plan changes after a twist / point of no return. |
| **F** | 2b | Complications converge; subplots pay pressure toward the low point. |
| **G** | 3 | The lowest point / final push; "all is lost" resolves into the climax setup. |
| **H** | 3 | Climax and resolution; central tension answered, new status quo. |

Each sequence should end on a **turn** that makes the next one necessary.

## Why it's powerful
It forces **local structure**. Instead of one giant escalation, you get eight satisfying
mini‑arcs. It also maps cleanly onto TV act‑breaks and onto novel "parts."

## Worked example — *Raiders of the Lost Ark* (sketch)
A: the idol heist (self‑contained mini‑movie establishing Indy). B: the mission briefing,
commit to finding the Ark. C: Nepal / the medallion. D: Cairo, digging. E: the Ark found /
"snakes" / apparent triumph flips. F: Nazis seize it; chase. G: captured, the island.
H: the Ark opened, the climax, resolution.

## Blank template
```text
TITLE:            CENTRAL TENSION:
SEQ A (Act 1) — goal / curtain:
SEQ B (Act 1) — goal / curtain (lock-in):
SEQ C (Act 2a) — goal / curtain:
SEQ D (Act 2a) — goal / curtain (→ midpoint):
SEQ E (Act 2b) — goal / curtain (midpoint reversal):
SEQ F (Act 2b) — goal / curtain:
SEQ G (Act 3) — goal / curtain (all is lost):
SEQ H (Act 3) — goal / curtain (climax + resolution):
```
