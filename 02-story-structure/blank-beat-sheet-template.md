# Blank Beat Sheet — Framework‑Agnostic Worksheet

Copy this whole file into your project and fill it in. It works with any spine in this
folder; pick your framework at the top and the mapped rows guide you.

---

## Universal beat grid (fill the % you're targeting)

> **⚑ unsure column:** flag any beat you haven't decided. These flags are exactly what the
> app's [**To‑Do / Unsure‑Decisions tab**](../app/README.md) collects so you can focus on
> open questions instead of losing them in the draft.
