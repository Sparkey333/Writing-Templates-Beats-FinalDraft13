# Outlining vs. Discovery Writing (Plotter vs. Pantser)

> "I'm an outliner for plot and a discovery writer for character."
> — a paraphrase of **Brandon Sanderson**'s approach, taught in his
> [BYU lectures](../08-resources/brandon-sanderson-resources.md).

There's no correct answer — only a spectrum, and a smart place on it for *you* and for *this
project*. This note helps you choose consciously.

## The spectrum
| | **Outliner (Plotter)** | **Discovery writer (Pantser)** |
|---|---|---|
| Plans | Detailed outline before drafting | Minimal; discovers by writing |
| Strength | Structure, foreshadowing, tight endings | Voice, surprise, organic character |
| Risk | Can feel mechanical; resists change | Saggy middles, messy revisions, dead ends |
| Method fit | [Snowflake](./snowflake-method.md), [9‑block](./nine-block-27-chapter.md) | Fichtean drafting, then structural revision |

## Sanderson's synthesis (the practical default)
- **Outline the plot skeleton** (major turns, the ending) so the structure holds and payoffs
  land — this is where "the [Sanderlanche](../04-worldbuilding/sanderson-worldbuilding-and-process.md)"
  climax comes from: converging plot lines you planned.
- **Discovery‑write the characters** — let them talk and surprise you within the planned plot.
- **Promise → Progress → Payoff:** whatever you promise the reader early (tone, a mystery, a
  relationship, a power), show *progress* on it, and *pay it off*. Track these three columns.

## A hybrid workflow (recommended)
1. **Snowflake steps 1–2** — lock the spine and the three disasters (outliner).
2. **Character dossiers** — but leave room; let voice emerge in drafting (discovery).
3. **Scene list for the next act only** — outline a few chapters ahead, not the whole book,
   so discoveries can feed forward.
4. **Flag "unsure" decisions** rather than forcing them — collect them in the
   [app's To‑Do tab](../app/README.md) and resolve deliberately.

## Choosing per project
- **Mystery, heist, time‑travel, tight thriller** → lean outliner (the machinery must click).
- **Character study, literary, slice‑of‑life** → lean discovery.
- **Epic fantasy / series** → outline the arc + world; discover scene‑to‑scene.
