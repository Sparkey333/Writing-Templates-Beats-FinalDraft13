# The 9‑Block / 27‑Chapter Method

> Three acts → nine blocks → twenty‑seven chapters. A clean, symmetrical skeleton.
> A community‑developed structure (Katytastic's popular "27 chapter method" video is the
> best‑known articulation).

If you like symmetry and want a **fill‑in‑the‑grid** approach, this is the most mechanical
(in a good way) novel skeleton: **3 acts × 3 blocks each = 9 blocks; 3 chapters per block =
27 chapters.** Each of the 27 slots has a named job.

## The grid
**ACT 1 — Setup**
- Block 1 (Setup): 1) **Introduction** · 2) inciting **Incident** · 3) **Immediate reaction**
- Block 2 (Reaction): 4) **Reaction** · 5) **Action** · 6) **Consequence**
- Block 3 (Development): 7) **Pressure** · 8) **Pinch** · 9) **Turn → Act 2** (leave the ordinary world)

**ACT 2 — Confrontation**
- Block 4 (Reaction): 10) **New world** · 11) **Fun & games** · 12) **Old contrast**
- Block 5 (**Midpoint**): 13) **Build** · 14) **Midpoint** (reversal) · 15) **Reversal fallout**
- Block 6 (Development): 16) **Reaction** · 17) **Action** · 18) **Dedication → all‑in**

**ACT 3 — Resolution**
- Block 7 (Reaction): 19) **Trials** · 20) **Pinch** (worst pressure) · 21) **Darkest / All is lost**
- Block 8 (Setup for finale): 22) **Power within** · 23) **Rising action** · 24) **Charge / plan**
- Block 9 (Resolution): 25) **Climax** · 26) **Falling action** · 27) **Resolution / new normal**

## Why writers like it
- **No blank pages:** you're filling 27 labeled slots.
- **Even pacing:** ~equal chapter counts per act prevent the bloated‑middle problem.
- **Scales:** collapse to 9 chapters (one per block) for a novella, or expand each slot to
  multiple chapters for an epic.

## How it maps
Slot 2 = Catalyst · Slot 9 = Break into Two · Slot 14 = Midpoint · Slot 21 = All Is Lost ·
Slot 25 = Climax — i.e., it's [Save the Cat](../02-story-structure/save-the-cat-15-beats.md)
on a fixed 27‑chapter lattice.
