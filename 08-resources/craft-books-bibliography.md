# Craft Books Bibliography

The books behind this repo's frameworks, organized by topic. Every book here is cited at the
bottom of its matching framework file — this is the consolidated shopping/reading list.

## Screenplay structure
- Blake Snyder, *Save the Cat! The Last Book on Screenwriting You'll Ever Need* (2005)
- Blake Snyder, *Save the Cat! Goes to the Movies* (2007) — the 5‑point finale expansion
- Syd Field, *Screenplay: The Foundations of Screenwriting* (1979)
- John Truby, *The Anatomy of Story: 22 Steps to Becoming a Master Storyteller* (2007)
- Paul Joseph Gulino, *Screenwriting: The Sequence Approach* (2004)
- Christopher Vogler, *The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers* (1992)

## Myth & the deep pattern
- Joseph Campbell, *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* (1949)

## Novel structure & process
- Randy Ingermanson, *How to Write a Novel Using the Snowflake Method*
- Jessica Brody, *Save the Cat! Writes a Novel* (2018)
- Dan Wells, "Seven‑Point Story Structure" (lecture series)
- Shawn Coyne, *The Story Grid* (2015)
- K.M. Weiland, *Creating Character Arcs*; *Structuring Your Novel*

## World‑building & magic systems
- J.R.R. Tolkien, "On Fairy‑Stories" (essay, 1947)
- J.R.R. Tolkien, *The Silmarillion* (1977, posthumous)
- J.R.R. Tolkien, *The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien*
- Brandon Sanderson, Laws of Magic essays (brandonsanderson.com — see
  [Sanderson resources](./brandon-sanderson-resources.md))

## Character
- Orson Scott Card, *Characters and Viewpoint* (the MICE quotient)
- K.M. Weiland, *Creating Character Arcs*

## Comics/graphic storytelling
- Scott McCloud, *Understanding Comics: The Invisible Art* (1993)

## Genre‑specific
- Gwen Hayes, *Romancing the Beat* (2016) — romance structure
- Ronald Knox, "A Detective Story Decalogue" (1929) — fair‑play mystery rules
- S.S. Van Dine, "Twenty Rules for Writing Detective Stories" (1928)

## Format & tools
- The Fountain open screenplay format spec — https://fountain.io/syntax
