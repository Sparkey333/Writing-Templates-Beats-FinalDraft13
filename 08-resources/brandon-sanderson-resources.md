# Brandon Sanderson — Full Resource Index

Every free, official resource from Brandon Sanderson on writing craft, world‑building, and
process — lectures, essays, and podcast — with verified links. His two craft contributions
get their own deep files: [Laws of Magic](../04-worldbuilding/sanderson-laws-of-magic.md) and
[Worldbuilding & Process](../04-worldbuilding/sanderson-worldbuilding-and-process.md).

## Free BYU lecture series (YouTube, full courses)
Sanderson has taught his "Creative Writing" course at Brigham Young University for years and
uploads full lecture series to YouTube for free, publicly.

- **2020 Creative Writing Lectures at BYU** — 13 lectures covering "the nuts and bolts of
  writing": plot, viewpoint, worldbuilding, short stories, characters, and publishing.
  **Playlist:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSH_xM-KC3Zv-79sVZTTj-YA6IAqh8qeQ
- **BYU class information & materials (official):**
  https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/byu-class-information
- Sanderson has also posted more recent lecture series in subsequent years — search
  "Brandon Sanderson" → **Playlists** on YouTube for the current full catalog; new years'
  lectures are added to his channel as he teaches them.

## Podcast
- ***Writing Excuses*** — a long‑running, influential writing‑craft podcast Sanderson
  co‑hosts (with rotating co‑hosts across seasons, historically including Dan Wells, Mary
  Robinette Kowal, Howard Tayler, and others). Episodes run short by design ("fifteen minutes
  long, because you're in a hurry, and we're not that smart").
  **Site:** https://writingexcuses.com/

## Books (craft‑adjacent, by Sanderson)
- The Cosmere novels themselves are the primary "worked examples" of his Laws in practice
  (*Mistborn*, *The Stormlight Archive*, *Elantris*, *Warbreaker*, and more) — see the
  [Top‑50 index](../07-reference-analyses/top-50-novels-beat-index.md) entries for *Mistborn*
  and *The Way of Kings*.

## A note on accuracy
Course offerings, playlist contents, and podcast co‑hosts change over time. The links above
were verified current as of this writing (2026); if anything's moved, brandonsanderson.com
and his YouTube channel's "Playlists" tab are the canonical, always‑current index.
