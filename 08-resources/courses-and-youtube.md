# Courses & YouTube — Beyond Sanderson

Free and paid learning resources for story structure, screenwriting, and world‑building.
(Sanderson's own lectures/podcast have a dedicated file:
[brandon-sanderson-resources.md](./brandon-sanderson-resources.md).)

## Free YouTube channels & series
- **Brandon Sanderson — 2020 BYU Creative Writing Lectures** — see
  [brandon-sanderson-resources.md](./brandon-sanderson-resources.md).
- **Dan Wells — "Seven‑Point Story Structure"** lecture series (widely posted; search the
  title directly — Wells co‑hosted *Writing Excuses* with Sanderson for years).
- **Hello Future Me** (YouTube) — structure and craft breakdowns, frequently covering the
  frameworks in this repo (Save the Cat, Story Circle, and others).
- **Just Write**, **Lessons from the Screenplay** — film‑structure and screenwriting analysis
  channels that do close beat‑sheet‑style breakdowns of produced films.

## Podcasts
- ***Writing Excuses*** — https://writingexcuses.com/ (Sanderson + rotating co‑hosts).
- Numerous screenwriting‑focused podcasts interview working writers about process; search by
  name for current, active shows since podcast landscapes shift quickly.

## Paid/structured courses
- **MasterClass** has hosted multiple author/screenwriter courses over the years (offerings
  change; check masterclass.com directly for current instructors).
- University continuing‑education and MFA programs — check local universities for
  extension/continuing‑ed creative writing and screenwriting courses.

## Software‑adjacent learning
- **Final Draft** publishes tutorials and webinars: https://www.finaldraft.com/
- **Save the Cat!** official site has beat‑sheet software and courses: https://savethecat.com/
- **Story Grid** publishes extensive free blog content on the Five Commandments:
  https://storygrid.com/

## How to use this list
Pair a course/lecture with the matching framework file in this repo — e.g., watch a Save the
Cat breakdown video alongside [save-the-cat-15-beats.md](../02-story-structure/save-the-cat-15-beats.md)
and try beat‑mapping a film you know well in real time.

> Course and channel offerings change often — this list is a starting point, not exhaustive.
> When in doubt, search the creator/topic directly for the current, active version.
