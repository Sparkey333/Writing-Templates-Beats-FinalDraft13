# Character Arc Template — Positive / Negative / Flat

Plot is what happens; **arc** is how the protagonist changes because of it. The cleanest model
(popularized by **K.M. Weiland**, *Creating Character Arcs*) ties arc to a **Lie the character
believes** vs. a **Truth** they need.

## The three arcs
| Arc | The movement | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| **Positive Change** | Believes a **Lie** → suffers → accepts the **Truth** | Growth; a better self |
| **Negative** | Believes a Lie → clings harder → **destroyed** by it | Fall/corruption (3 sub‑types below) |
| **Flat (Steadfast)** | *Holds* the Truth → the **world** changes around them | The hero changes *others* |

**Negative sub‑types:** *Disillusionment* (learns a truth, but it's a bleak one) ·
*Fall* (rejects the truth, embraces the lie — tragedy) · *Corruption* (had the truth,
chooses the lie).

## The positive arc, beat by beat (maps to Three‑Act)
| Story position | Inner arc beat |
|---|---|
| Opening | Lives comfortably in **the Lie**; has a **Want** rooted in it. |
| Inciting/Act 1 turn | The **Need** (Truth) is glimpsed; Want and Need start to diverge. |
| Midpoint | A **Moment of Truth** — sees the Lie clearly for the first time; can't unsee it. |
| All Is Lost (~75%) | The Lie fails catastrophically; clinging to Want costs everything. |
| Climax | **Chooses the Truth/Need over the Want** — proves change through action. |
| Final image | Lives the Truth; the opposite of the opening. |

> The **Want vs. Need** split is the spine: usually the hero must **give up the Want to gain
> the Need** (or realize the Want was the wrong goal all along).

## Worked examples
- **Positive:** *Groundhog Day* — Lie: "I'm the center of the universe" → Truth: selflessness.
- **Flat/Steadfast:** *Paddington*, most James Bond, Atticus Finch — the hero holds a value
  and reforms the world/others around them.
- **Negative (Corruption):** *Breaking Bad* — Walter White chooses the lie, arc by arc.

## Blank template
```text
CHARACTER:                    ARC TYPE: positive / negative(_______) / flat
THE LIE they believe:
THE TRUTH they need:
THE WANT (external goal, rooted in the Lie):
THE NEED (internal, = the Truth):
GHOST (why they believe the Lie):

BEATS
  Opening — living the Lie:
  Act 1 turn — Truth glimpsed:
  Midpoint — Moment of Truth:
  All Is Lost — the Lie fails:
  Climax — the choice (Truth over Want):
  Final image — living the Truth:

CHECK: Does the external plot FORCE the internal change? Do Want and Need collide at the climax?
```
