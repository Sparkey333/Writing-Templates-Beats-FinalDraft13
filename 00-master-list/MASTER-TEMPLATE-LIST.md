# The Master Template List

The compendium's central catalog. Three tiers:

1. **The Core 20** — a curated, battle‑tested set of story‑structure, novel, character,
   world, and genre frameworks that together cover ~everything you'll reach for.
2. **Requested Additions** — the specific templates and study targets you asked to merge in
   (Toonami/shōnen, Snowflake, Tolkien world‑building, Top‑50 novel beats, the Kingkiller
   Chronicle, Brandon Sanderson's process, and the *Matrix* / *Inception* screenplays).
3. **10 Bonus Genre Kits** — ten more templates picked to round out the theme.

Everything below links to a full working file. ✅ = built in this repo.

## Quick chooser — "which framework do I grab?"

- **I just need to outline something, fast** → Three‑Act + [Save the Cat](../02-story-structure/save-the-cat-15-beats.md).
- **Character transformation is the point** → [Story Circle](../02-story-structure/story-circle.md) or [Truby 22](../02-story-structure/truby-22-steps.md).
- **I know my ending, not my middle** → [Seven‑Point](../02-story-structure/seven-point-structure.md).
- **Writing a novel and staring at a blank page** → [Snowflake](../03-novel-methods/snowflake-method.md).
- **Building a world/magic system** → [Sanderson's Laws](../04-worldbuilding/sanderson-laws-of-magic.md) + [world questionnaire](../04-worldbuilding/worldbuilding-master-questionnaire.md).
- **Screenplay** → [format spec](../01-screenplay/formatting/screenplay-format-spec.md) + a [Fountain template](../01-screenplay/templates/).
- **Escalating action serial / anime** → [Toonami/Shōnen kit](../06-genre-templates/toonami-shonen-powerup-arc.md).
- **Studying how a masterwork is built** → [Kingkiller analysis](../07-reference-analyses/kingkiller-chronicle-structure.md), the [Matrix](../01-screenplay/beat-sheets/the-matrix-beat-sheet.md)/[Inception](../01-screenplay/beat-sheets/inception-beat-sheet.md) beat sheets, [Top‑50 index](../07-reference-analyses/top-50-novels-beat-index.md).
